The standard

Six things a generated site does. Six things yours will not.

Most small business websites now come out of the same handful of tools, and they share the same tells. You have seen them without knowing their names. Below: the six tells, each one shown beside what you get instead. Then two whole sites you can scroll, one generated and one finished. Then this page measured against the list.

    Rounded everything

    Pill buttons, softened cards, corners rounded because the tool's default was rounded. It is the single fastest way to spot a template.
