The standard

Six things a generated site does. Six things yours will not.

Most small business websites now come out of the same handful of tools, and they share the same tells. You have seen them without knowing their names. Here they are, named, beside what you get instead. The same list is applied to this page, and the numbers are underneath.

  1. 01

    Rounded everything

    Pill buttons, softened cards, corners rounded because the tool's default was rounded. It is the single fastest way to spot a template.

    Sharp geometry, decided

    Square corners on every element, including the favicon in the browser tab. Nothing on the page is a leftover setting.

  2. 02

    Gradients as decoration

    Colour fading into other colour behind the headline because the empty space made someone nervous.

    One accent, used on purpose

    A single colour that marks what matters: the price, the button, the thing to read next. Everywhere else, restraint.

  3. 03

    Whatever font the computer had

    A system font stack is the absence of a decision. Your brand looks different on every phone and the same as every other site.

    A chosen pair of typefaces

    A display face and a reading face, picked for the business, served from your own site so nothing is fetched from Google to render your name.

  4. 04

    The section march

    Seven to nine near identical blocks in a row: heading, three cards, button, repeat. Scroll and nothing changes.

    Four sections that each say a thing

    Fewer, longer, and different from one another. This page has four. Yours will have as many as it needs and not one more.

  5. 05

    Card soup

    Every idea in its own floating box with its own drop shadow, so nothing on the page is more important than anything else.

    Hierarchy you can feel

    Rules, borders and type size do the work. When something is in a box on your page, it is because it earned one.

  6. 06

    Copy that describes a process

    "We leverage a client centric approach to deliver solutions." Nobody says that out loud, and nobody reads it.

    Words a customer would actually say

    Your prices, your town, your hours, the question people ring you with. Specific is the whole difference, and it cannot be templated.
