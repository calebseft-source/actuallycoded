The correction above, then a professional grade coating laid in a dust free bay and cured overnight. Water beads for years instead of months, and washing becomes a rinse. Three days, guaranteed for five years.

Prices include tax and are for cars up to a mid size SUV. Trucks, vans and anything with a roof box are quoted after a look, in writing, before the booking is taken.

How it goes

Four steps. Nobody skips the first two.

The order matters more than the products. Most of what passes for detailing is step four done on top of dirt.

  1. Wash and decontaminate. Foam to lift the grit, two buckets so it never goes back on, then iron and tar removed chemically and a clay bar over every panel. The paint is clean enough to touch before any machine does.
  2. Inspect under the light. A swirl finder lamp over every panel, a paint depth gauge on the edges, and a written note of anything that will not come out. You see the note before the polisher starts.
  3. Correct, then refine. A cutting pad takes the defects out. A finishing pad takes the haze the cutting pad left. Each panel is wiped with alcohol and checked again before the next.
  4. Protect, and leave it alone. Coating or sealant, laid thin, cured in the bay overnight. The car is not driven, washed or rained on until it is ready, which is the only part the shop cannot promise for you.

Coating

Water that will not sit still.

A coated panel does not hold water. It beads, then it runs, and it takes the dirt with it. That is the whole point, and it is why a coated car washes in a quarter of the time for as long as the coating lasts.

The bay

Closed door, one car, and a light that shows everything.

The bay is sealed while a car is in it. No second car, no walk ins, no door open to the road. The lights are colour matched to daylight so a panel that looks right in the bay looks right in the sun.

  1. Drop off by nine. The wash starts at nine sharp because the correction needs the whole day after it.
  2. Empty the car first. Anything left inside is boxed and handed back. Nothing is thrown away and nothing is opened.
  3. Collection is by text. You get a message with three photos under the light when it is done, and you collect when it suits you.

Before you book

Asked most weeks.

Is a ceramic coating worth it on a daily driver?

If you wash the car yourself, yes, because every wash gets faster and nothing sticks. If the car lives at a touchless car wash, a sealant twice a year does most of the same job for a tenth of the price, and the shop will say so.

Will correction take out every scratch?

No. Anything your fingernail catches in is through the clear coat and polishing cannot bring it back. It gets written down at the inspection, with a photo, before any machine touches the car.

Can I wait with the car?

Not in the bay. The door is closed while a car is in and the correction takes a day. Drop it off by nine and collect it when the text with the photos comes through.
