The fire

Three things the cook would say if you asked.

We light at four

Oak takes ninety minutes to become the kind of heat that sears without scorching. That is why the first table is at half past five and not a minute before.

Oak only

Split and dried for a year. Nothing softwood, nothing treated, nothing from a bag. You can taste the difference and so can we.

Nothing waits under a lamp

A plate leaves the pass the moment it is done, which is why tables of six sometimes eat in two waves. Ask and we will tell you which wave you are in.

Before you come in

Asked most weeks.

Do I have to book?

For a table on Friday or Saturday, yes, and a text with the night and party size gets a time back the same day. The counter is never booked. Walk in and take the next seats that free up.

Is there anything for someone who does not eat meat?

Half the oven and all of the sides. The mushroom and taleggio, the coal roasted vegetables, the potatoes in the ash and the bitter leaves make a full dinner, and the kitchen will say so without being asked.

Why are there no reviews, ratings, or awards on this page?

Because Emberline does not exist. It is a fictional restaurant built by actuallycoded as a portfolio concept, so this page carries no invented testimonials, star ratings, or press. Everything on it is design and copy, shown honestly as such.

Can I actually book here?

No. There is no room, no oven, and no fire. The form on this page is a non submitting interaction demo that clears itself and keeps nothing. If you want a real website built this way, that is what actuallycoded does.
