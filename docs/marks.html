The mark, and three alternates.

Same discipline as the last one: a 100 by 100 grid, exact strokes, no curve anywhere, at most one diagonal, and nothing that says code. Each is shown as the header mark beside the wordmark, as a tile, and at favicon size, on dark and on light. The monogram is wired in across the site. The three geometric alternates are kept in case the monogram reads as too close to the old brand.
