# Hollis Barbershop concept image sources

The Hollis Barbershop page is explicitly labeled as a fictional portfolio concept.
Its photographs are used for art direction only. They do not depict Hollis
Barbershop, which does not exist, and they do not represent completed client work.

All source photographs were downloaded from Pexels and may be used and modified
under the Pexels license: https://www.pexels.com/license/

- `hero.jpg` and its optimized `hero.webp` derivative, wal_:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/37764947/
- `room.jpg`, gromakova:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/9146943/
- `craft.jpg`, Izzet Cakalli:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/12464841/
- `tools.jpg`, nickoloui:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/1319459/

The repository copies are cropped, resized, and optimized derivatives. Original
downloads are kept outside the published site in the project workspace.

## Checked before use

The hero's back shelf holds product bottles. At full resolution the largest label
reads as a partial brand name at a few pixels tall; at the 1800 pixel width the
site serves, behind the scrim, it is not legible. Judged acceptable. The crop was
biased left to keep the chairs and push the shelf toward the edge.

## Rejected on inspection

- **13058812** had a large BANDIDO WANTED sign over the counter.
- **14011984** was a portrait of a shop front with the owner's name painted on the
  window in letters a foot high.
- **7697196** showed product cans with legible labels.
