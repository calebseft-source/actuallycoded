# Northline Dental concept image sources

The Northline Dental page is explicitly labeled as a fictional portfolio concept.
Its photographs are used for art direction only. They do not depict Northline
Dental, which does not exist, and they do not represent completed client work.

All source photographs were downloaded from Pexels and may be used and modified
under the Pexels license: https://www.pexels.com/license/

- `hero.jpg` and its optimized `hero.webp` derivative, Pavel Danilyuk:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/6812461/
- `detail.jpg`, Pavel Danilyuk:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/6809639/
- `room.jpg`, Tima Miroshnichenko:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/5355920/

The repository copies are cropped, resized, and optimized derivatives. Original
downloads are kept outside the published site in the project workspace.

## Checked before use

Every frame was opened and looked at rather than trusted from its caption. None
of the three carries a readable brand name, logo, signage, product packaging, or
identifiable practice marking, which is a requirement for a fictional business:
a recognisable real brand in the photography would be both a trademark problem
and a contradiction of the fictional label.
