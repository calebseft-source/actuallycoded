# Kestrel Electric concept image sources

The Kestrel Electric page is explicitly labeled as a fictional portfolio concept.
Its photographs are used for art direction only. They do not depict Kestrel
Electric, which does not exist, and they do not represent completed client work.

All source photographs were downloaded from Pexels and may be used and modified
under the Pexels license: https://www.pexels.com/license/

- `hero.jpg` and its optimized `hero.webp` derivative, Kathleen Austin Kuhn:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/32497160/
- `work.jpg`, Audy of Course:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/20500461/
- `detail.jpg`, Sphinxx69:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/38292956/

The repository copies are cropped, resized, and optimized derivatives. Original
downloads are kept outside the published site in the project workspace.

## Rejected on inspection

Every candidate was opened and looked at rather than trusted from its caption.

- **27928762** carried an equipment label reading AEPL-010.
- **17063686** had Chinese street signage in the background.
- **28950842** showed Schneider and L&T logos on every breaker.
- **14319099** featured a Fluke multimeter, unmistakable by its yellow case.
- **4981794** had the word Marigold printed on the glove.
- **7285965** read as hobby electronics, a soldering station, not an electrician.
