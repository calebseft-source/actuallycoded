# Fieldnote Coffee concept image sources

The Fieldnote Coffee page is explicitly labeled as a fictional portfolio concept.
Its photographs are used for art direction only. They do not depict Fieldnote
Coffee, which does not exist, and they do not represent completed client work.

All source photographs were downloaded from Pexels and may be used and modified
under the Pexels license: https://www.pexels.com/license/

- `hero.jpg` and its optimized `hero.webp` derivative, Arda Kaykisiz:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/plants-in-a-restaurant-18721993/
- `counter.jpg`, Yusuf Kaya:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/20117299/
- `room.jpg`, Lisa Fotios:
  https://www.pexels.com/photo/cozy-cafe-interior-with-urban-view-34353697/

The repository copies are cropped, resized, and optimized derivatives. Original
downloads are kept outside the published site in the project workspace.

## Rejected candidates, and why

Worth recording, because the failure mode here is trusting a caption instead of
opening the image.

- **Pexels 1855214** was discarded after being downloaded. The caption described
  a generic coffee shop; the frame showed Starbucks menu boards reading BLONDE
  ROAST and branded packaging on the counter. Unusable for a fictional business
  and a trademark problem regardless.
- **Pexels 7601665** was discarded for two reasons: portrait orientation where a
  landscape hero was needed, and legible chalkboard menu text in Turkish, which
  would have contradicted an Orlando setting.
- **Pexels 34353697** was too tall to serve as the hero, so it was kept and used
  as the portrait room image instead.

The hero search was eventually run with `?orientation=landscape` rather than
filtering by eye afterwards.
